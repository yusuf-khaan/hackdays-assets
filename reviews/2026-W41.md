## What changed in the repo
- **One big change: `tools/lint.js`**, a text check that `tools/carousel.js` and `tools/render.js` now run on every render: WARN when two text elements overlap, and WARN on unquoted `font-family:Serif`. Tested on a deliberately broken story and deck (both caught), and on all 11 decks and 12 stories from 10-11 Oct: no false overlap warnings; the Serif check correctly flags the old hackdays stories that use it. `data-overlap-ok` marks a deliberate overlay.
- Bug fix: `carousel.js` crashed on the contact sheet for decks of 5 or fewer slides (fractional viewport height). Fixed with `Math.ceil`, tested on a 2-slide deck.
- RUN.md "Making a story": the overlap rule now points to the new WARN.
- All four learnings files rewritten: owner rules moved into Avoid, unrun or unmeasurable experiments closed, 2-3 experiments per account opened, backlogs refilled (10+ each), verse backlog turned into story ideas and reel suggestions.
- Changelog ideas checked: the WebFetch note was already in RUN.md "Known environment issues"; the overlap and Serif ideas are done; the emoji note stays a rule in learnings.
