05 / 08
the 3-line scope
Write your idea in 3 lines.
1
One userNot "students". Which student, doing what?
2
One taskThe single job your project does for them.
3
One "done" momentWhat you can show working, start to finish.
Here's the day-1 idea, rewritten…
06 / 08
git diff idea.md
idea.md-1 +3
An AI agent that manages your whole college life.
User: a 2nd-year who misses deadlines posted in class groups. Task: read the notice, add the deadline to their calendar. Done: a reminder lands 24 hours before it's due.
Same spirit. One loop. An Agents track project you can actually finish.
And that changes what your demo looks like…
07 / 08
the payoff
One loop that works
beats six that
half-work.
Small scope means your 16 days go into making it run end to end, so your public repo and demo video show the real thing, not a promise.
