Explained in 4 steps
// rag · retrieval-augmented generation

Give your model
an open book.

A model only knows what it was trained on. RAG lets it look things up in your data before it answers.
01
index()
Split your docs into chunks and turn each into an embedding, stored in a vector database.
02
retrieve(query)
Embed the question and fetch the chunks that are closest in meaning.
03
augment(prompt)
Put those chunks into the prompt as context, with an instruction to answer from them.
04
generate() + cite
The model answers grounded in your data and can point to where it came from.

Retrieval quality is the whole game. Bad chunks in, confident nonsense out.
