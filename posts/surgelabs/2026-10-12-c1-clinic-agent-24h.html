any reply, any wording

// the reveal

Not the AI. A template approved in advance.

template: appointment_reminder
Hi {{1}}, your appointment is on {{2}} at {{3}}. Reply 1 to confirm, 2 to reschedule.
patient replies “2”
window reopens → agent
The patient’s reply opens a new 24-hour window, and the agent takes over again. Plan the templates before launch, not after.
