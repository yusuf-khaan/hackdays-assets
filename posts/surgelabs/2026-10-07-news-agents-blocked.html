AI agents can shop now.
Websites are blocking them.

Personal agents like Meta's Muse and ChatGPT's Dots are running into sites that block them. Three different reasons:
why
what's happening
on purpose
Amazon deliberately blocks Muse from making purchases.
by accident
Walmart's blocks appear tied to human-verification checks, not policy.
pay to enter
Yelp requires a paid data licence for agent access.
the fix?
Meta, Walmart, Stripe, Cloudflare and others back an open standard for agent-to-business commerce.
What it means for builders

Don't build agents that only scrape. Plan for blocks, prefer official APIs and open protocols, and always have a fallback to a human.
