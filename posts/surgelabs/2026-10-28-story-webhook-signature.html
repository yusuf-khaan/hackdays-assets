Pattern · Webhooks

Anyone can POST to your webhook URL. How do you know it was Meta?Three steps, before you trust a single byte.

1
Hash the raw bodyHMAC-SHA256 of the body exactly as received, before parsing the JSON, keyed with your app secret.
2
Compare to the headerX-Hub-Signature-256 carries sha256=<hex>. Compare in constant time.
3
Mismatch? Reject itReturn an error and ignore the payload.
