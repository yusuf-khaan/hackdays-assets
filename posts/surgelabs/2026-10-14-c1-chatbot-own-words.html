// the ruling

Rejected. The bot’s words were the company’s words.

“no reason why [he] should know that one section of Air Canada’s webpage is accurate, and another is not.”
tribunal decision, as quoted by CTV News
The company is responsible for everything on its site, chatbot included. It was ordered to pay a partial refund.

// under the hood · a safer agent

Answer from your policy, or hand it to a human.

OWASP’s LLM Top 10 calls this risk “excessive agency” and recommends human approval for high-impact actions.

08 / 08
// lab notes · before your agent talks to customers

Treat it like a new hire who can’t invent policy.

[01]Load the real policy. Answer only from it.
[02]Not in the docs? Hand off, don’t guess.
[03]Money promises need a human yes.
And log every answer, so you can see what it said.
Save this before you switch your bot on.
