■ SURGELABS
Pattern of the day

You tapped Pay twice. Were you charged twice?Not if the app sends an idempotency key: one ID per intended action.

# tap 1
POST /pay key=a7f3
→ charged
# tap 2, same key
POST /pay key=a7f3
→ same result, no new charge
Retries become safe. Our own posting pipeline uses one key per post, so a retry never posts twice.
