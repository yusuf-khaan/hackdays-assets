Wrong number

Your computer says 0.1 + 0.2 is not 0.3.It is not a bug. It is how decimals are stored.

JavaScript console
> 0.1 + 0.2
0.30000000000000004
> 0.1 + 0.2 === 0.3
false
Why: 0.1 and 0.2 have no exact binary form, so they are stored as close approximations (IEEE 754 doubles). The tiny error shows up in the sum.
