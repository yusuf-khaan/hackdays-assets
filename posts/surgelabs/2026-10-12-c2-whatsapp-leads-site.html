03 / 08
#1

The page takes too long to show up.

0 s1 s2 s3 s
53%
of mobile visits are abandoned when a page takes longer than 3 seconds to load.Google research, 2016
Fix: compress the big photos, drop scripts you don’t use.
Say they wait. Now they read the first line…

04 / 08
#2

The first screen doesn’t say what you do.

before
Welcome to our website
Quality you can trust
after
Physiotherapy at home. Same-week visits.
What, for whom, and the next step: one tap to chat.
Now they want to tap. Their thumb has other ideas…

05 / 08
#3

Buttons too small for a thumb.

before
icons crammed together
after
Chat on WhatsApp
one wide button
Google’s Lighthouse guideline: tap targets at least 48 × 48 px, with space between them.
So they try calling instead. They can’t…
SurgeLabs_
06 / 08
#4

The number is a picture, not a link.

// before: copy it by hand, switch apps, paste
<img src="phone-number.png">
// after: one tap
<a href="tel:+91XXXXXXXXXX">Call</a>
A number inside an image can’t be tapped, copied or read by a screen reader.
Which leaves the one button built for this. And it’s broken too…
SurgeLabs_
07 / 08
#5

The WhatsApp link has the wrong number format.

broken
wa.me/+91 0XXXXX-XXXXX
works
wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hi
Full international number: no +, no leading 0, no spaces or dashes. Add ?text= so the chat opens with a first line ready.
