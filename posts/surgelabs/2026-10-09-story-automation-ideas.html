Three automations
almost any business can use.

Pick the task your team repeats every day. Start there.
01

Invoices

on invoice_received → extract fields
→ match to order → flag mismatches for a human
02

New leads

on form_submitted → log to CRM
→ route to the right person → draft a follow-up
03

Support inbox

on email_received → classify
→ answer the FAQs → escalate the rest, with context
Automate the routine, keep people on the exceptions. That's how we build backend automation and agent systems.
