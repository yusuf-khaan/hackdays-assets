// the usual causes, and the fix

Three feelings. Three fixes.

wait · lcp
Compress the hero image. Don’t lazy-load it.
jump · cls
Give images width and height. Reserve space for banners and embeds.
freeze · inp
Cut heavy third-party scripts. Break long tasks into small ones.
Source: Google’s web.dev guides on LCP, CLS and INP.
