Mahishasura, the buffalo-demon.
The Devi Mahatmya, part of the Markandeya Purana, says devas and asuras fought for a full hundred years. He won, and took Indra’s throne.

He kept changing shape.
Illustration
The text says he took one form after another. At last she defeated him. Many traditions say the battle ran nine nights, and she won on the tenth day.
Nine nights. Then a tenth day. Sound familiar?
07 / 08
नवरात्रि
Navratri.
Nine nights for the goddess, then Vijayadashami, the “tenth day of victory”. She is hailed as Mahishasuramardini: slayer of Mahishasura.
11 OCT · NIGHT 120 OCT · DAY 10
Versions differ: in much of North India, the tenth day, Dussehra, also marks Rama’s victory over Ravana.
And the demon’s name is still on India’s map…
08 / 08
Mysuru.
By tradition, the city is named after him: Mahishuru, “Mahisha’s town”. Local legend says the goddess, as Chamundeshwari, defeated him on Chamundi Hill.
