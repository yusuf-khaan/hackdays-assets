The epic says · Mahabharata
The Gita opens with a question.The blind king Dhritarashtra asks Sanjaya what his sons and the Pandavas did on the field of Kurukshetra.
धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे समवेता युयुत्सवः ।
मामकाः पाण्डवाश्चैव किमकुर्वत सञ्जय ॥
dharmakṣetre kurukṣetre samavetā yuyutsavah
māmakāh pāṇḍavāścaiva kim akurvata sañjaya
Bhagavad Gita 1.1
