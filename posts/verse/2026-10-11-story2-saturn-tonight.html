TONIGHT'S SKY · INDIA
That steady "star" in the east after dark? It's Saturn.And this year its rings are playing a trick on you.
ILLUSTRATION
WHY NOW

On 4 Oct, Earth passed between the Sun and Saturn. It is at its brightest of 2026, up from dusk to dawn.

THE TRICK

Its rings are turned almost edge-on to us this year. In a small telescope they look like a thin line.

SPOT IT

Look east to south-east after dark. Planets shine steady; stars twinkle.
