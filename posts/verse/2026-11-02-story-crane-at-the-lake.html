Four Pandavas drank from the lake. A crane had asked them to answer first.The epic says (Mahabharata, Vana Parva): the crane was a Yaksha. Only Yudhishthira stopped to answer. Retellings differ in detail.
Illustration
1He answers the Yaksha's questions
2The Yaksha offers to revive one brother
3He chooses Nakula. All four rise.
Sources: Wikipedia (Yaksha Prashna); Mahabharata, Vana Parva, section CCCXI (sacred-texts.com)
