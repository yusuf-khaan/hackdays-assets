Word origin
Shampoo began as an order in Hindi.You say it every day. It was once a command.
चाँपो
Hindi
→
chāmpo
"press!"
→
shampoo
English

Chāmpo is the command form of chāmpnā, "to press" or "to knead".

English borrowed it for a massage first. Washing hair came later.
