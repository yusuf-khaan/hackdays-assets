## Avoid
- Unquoted `font-family:Serif` in CSS: it is the generic `serif` keyword and renders Times, not Instrument Serif. Always write `font-family:"Serif"` (found 2026-10-11; earlier hackdays posts render Times).
- Formats already used as single posts (log.md): terminal boot screen, character-select, timeline/roadmap, myth vs fact, patch notes. Reuse them only as slides inside richer carousels.
