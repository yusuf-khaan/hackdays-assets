## Facts (source of truth; never invent beyond these)
- Homingo Hack Days: national-scale, online AI Engineering hackathon for students and early-career developers across India. Edition 01.
- Site: hackathon.homingo.co.in. Online, open to all of India. Solo or teams of 2–4.
- Tracks: Agents (autonomous agents that plan, use tools and finish real tasks), Automation (pipelines and workflows that remove busywork from real operations), LLM apps (products on large language models, shipped for real users).
- Edition 01 timeline (2026, IST): Registration 1 Oct – 15 Nov (closes 15 Nov 11:59 PM IST). Team formation until 18 Nov. Kick-off and problem statements 21 Nov. Build phase 21 Nov – 6 Dec. Submissions 4 – 6 Dec (repo, demo video, deck). Shortlisting 7 – 12 Dec. Finals and online demo day 19 Dec. Results and certificates 21 – 31 Dec.
- Rewards: cash prize pool for the top 3 (amount NOT announced: say "cash prize pool", never a number). Recommendation Certificate from SurgeLabs for the top 3. Verifiable participation certificate (unique ID + QR) for everyone who submits.
- Perks: internship/freelance opportunities with SurgeLabs, fast-track interview consideration, Homingo Stores access, national spotlight for winners, portfolio reviews, LinkedIn recommendations, alumni community, early access to future editions.
- Mentors: fully online and flexible (video calls, chat, async feedback; no fixed hours). Mentors are never named or shown. No cash for mentors; recognition: mentor certificate, LinkedIn recommendation from SurgeLabs, early access to talent, Mentor → Senior Mentor → Judge.
- Part of the Homingo family, powered by SurgeLabs. The hackathon is the hero.
- Tagline: "Code till dawn. Ship by sunrise."
