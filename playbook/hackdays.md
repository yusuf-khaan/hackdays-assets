# @homingo.hackdays playbook

Account: handle `homingo.hackdays`. Find its id with `list_accounts` (ids changed on 2026-10-10; never rely on a stored id).

## What the account is for
Promotion of Homingo Hack Days, its use cases and benefits, daily tech news, world changes and tech affairs, always tied back to builders and AI engineering where it fits.

## Facts (source of truth; never invent beyond these)
- Homingo Hack Days: national-scale, online AI Engineering hackathon for students (school and college, 16+) and early-career developers across India. Edition 01.
- Site: hackathon.homingo.co.in. Online, open to all of India. Solo or teams of 2–4.
- Tracks: Agents (autonomous agents that plan, use tools and finish real tasks), Automation (pipelines and workflows that remove busywork from real operations), LLM apps (products on large language models, shipped for real users).
- Edition 01 timeline (2026, IST): Registration 1 Oct – 15 Nov (closes 15 Nov 11:59 PM IST). Team formation until 18 Nov. Kick-off and problem statements 21 Nov. Build phase 21 Nov – 6 Dec. Submissions 4 – 6 Dec (repo, demo video, deck). Shortlisting 7 – 12 Dec. Finals & review day 19 Dec (online; there is no separate demo day). Results and certificates 21 – 31 Dec.
- Rewards: ₹5,000 cash prize to the winner (the winner only; no cash for 2nd or 3rd). Recommendation Certificate from SurgeLabs for the top 3. Verifiable participation certificate (unique ID + QR) for everyone who submits.
- Perks: internship/freelance opportunities with SurgeLabs, fast-track interview consideration, Homingo Stores access, national spotlight for winners, portfolio reviews, LinkedIn recommendations, alumni community, early access to future editions.
- Mentors: fully online and flexible (video calls, chat, async feedback; no fixed hours). Mentors are never named or shown. No cash for mentors; recognition: mentor certificate, LinkedIn recommendation from SurgeLabs, early access to talent, Mentor → Senior Mentor → Judge.
- Part of the Homingo family, powered by SurgeLabs. The hackathon is the hero.
- Tagline: "Code till dawn. Ship by sunrise."

## Feed carousels (2 a day; see CAROUSEL.md)
Every feed post is a carousel. Two different categories a day; cover all four every two days; the phase calendar decides the weighting (registration conversion until 15 Nov leans on Countdown and How it works; the build phase leans on Project ideas and Builder tips).
- **Countdown and deadlines** (`countdown`): days left to register, key dates, what to do before each date. Turn the countdown into a story ("14 days. Here's how a solo builder can ship an agent in that time.") rather than a bare number.
- **Project ideas** (`ideas`): what to build in Agents, Automation and LLM apps. Concrete, scoped ideas with the problem, the user, the core loop, and a stretch goal. Tie to recent launches when TRENDS.md finds a strong one.
- **How it works** (`how`): teams, registration, submission (repo, demo video and deck), review, the prize, certificates, mentors, the timeline. Only the facts above.
- **Builder tips** (`tips`): scoping a project, recording a good demo video, a clean README, prompting, agent design, evaluation, shipping fast.
Slide formats to borrow from (mix inside one carousel; invent new ones too): terminal boot screen · patch notes · error page · git log · character-select · countdown clock · checklist / todo.md · code diff · chat thread · news card with source · myth vs fact · FAQ · timeline · comparison table.

## Trend radar (search terms for TRENDS.md)
New AI model or agent launches, developer tools going viral, big AI news from the last 48 hours, hackathon or student-developer stories trending in India, "everyone is building X". Angle: "what you could build with it for Hack Days".

## Visual system
- 1080×1350 JPEG carousel slides. Background #0A0A0B with subtle grain. Accents: Hermès orange #F37021 (leads), blue #1E9BEA (supports). White text; muted grey #8A8A93; charcoal surfaces #141416, lines #27272A.
- Type: "Serif" (Instrument Serif) for headlines, one key word in italic orange; "Sans" (Inter) for body and letter-spaced uppercase labels; "Mono" (JetBrains Mono) for terminal/code.
- Signature motif: orange rising half-sun over a blue glowing cursor line.
- Premium, editorial, generous space. No neon, no stock gradients, no emoji in the image, no clip-art.
- Reference: posts/hackdays/2026-10-06-edition01-announce.html (copy its structure; link ../../tools/fonts.css).

## Stories (two per day, 1080×1920)
Stories cover the same wide range as the feed, not one recurring type. Rotate across all of these, never the same category two days running, and check the last 7 days of stories so the mix stays varied:
- Tech affairs and news: one verified headline of the day (last 48 hours, named source), with a one-line "what this means for builders".
- Benefits: one reward or perk per story (₹5,000 to the winner, Recommendation Certificate, verifiable certificate, internships/freelance with SurgeLabs, fast-track interviews, portfolio reviews, alumni community).
- Promotion: why join, who it's for, "online, all of India, solo or teams of 2–4", registration call to action.
- Track spotlights: Agents, Automation, LLM apps, with an example of the kind of thing to build.
- Builder tips: a short practical tip (prompting, agent design, shipping a demo, writing a good README, demo video).
- Myth vs fact / FAQ: "Do I need a team?", "Is it online?", "Beginners welcome?" (answer only from the facts above).
- Mentors: the mentor call, how mentoring works (online, async, no fixed hours).
- Timeline: what happens next and when.
- Culture and fun: developer humour, "this or that" (no poll sticker; just the visual), late-night building mood, code till dawn.
- New-post teaser: points to today's feed post.
- Countdowns: at most twice a week until 8 Nov; daily from 9 Nov to 15 Nov ("<N> days left · closes 15 Nov, 11:59 PM IST"; on 15 Nov "Last day. Closes tonight 11:59 PM IST."). After 15 Nov, occasional countdowns to the next milestone (team formation ends 18 Nov, kick-off 21 Nov, submissions close 6 Dec, finals & review day 19 Dec).
Vary the visual format too (terminal, serif statement, card, list, clock, chat bubble, diff). Include hackathon.homingo.co.in or "Link in bio" when the story promotes registration.
