Sell the feeling (space, money, letting go, a good find) and the ease (one tap on Sell, free listing, buyers chat or call you directly). Slides carry a story with open loops (CAROUSEL.md still applies: hook gate, swipe test, payoff, one CTA), but the payoff is emotional or witty, not a checklist.
