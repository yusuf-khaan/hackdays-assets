# @surgelabs_ playbook

Account: handle `surgelabs_`. Find its id with `list_accounts` (ids changed on 2026-10-10; never rely on a stored id).

## What the account is for
- Promote Homingo Hack Days (SurgeLabs powers it; point people to @homingo.hackdays).
- SurgeLabs itself: an AI and IT consultancy focused on autonomous agent systems, backend automation and scalable infrastructure. Explain what these are, how they help businesses, and how SurgeLabs approaches them.
- World-leading tech news and tech affairs: AI models and launches, agents, infrastructure, big-tech moves, policy that affects builders and businesses.
- Useful explainers: concepts (RAG, agents, MCP, vector databases, queues, observability), patterns, short how-it-works breakdowns.

## Rules
- Never invent clients, case studies, numbers, partnerships, prices or product features. Describe SurgeLabs only at the level above. If a post needs specifics that aren't here, make it an explainer instead.
- Tech news must be from the last 48 hours, confirmed with web search from reputable sources, and summarised in our own words. Put "Source: <publication>" in the caption. If nothing is solid, post an explainer instead.
- Never present opinion as fact; keep political topics neutral.

## Feed carousels (2 a day; see CAROUSEL.md)
Every feed post is a carousel, designed in the lab look of playbook/surgelabs-lab.md. Two different categories a day; cover all four every two days. Hack Days promotion and world tech news ride inside these categories (a news carousel is "AI agents and automation" or "Behind the build" through our lens; promote @homingo.hackdays on the last slide when it fits, more often in the last 10 days before 15 Nov).
- **AI agents and automation** (`agents`): what an agent can do for a business; before and after an automation. Scenarios are illustrative and must say so ("Imagine a clinic that…", "A typical shop…"); never present them as SurgeLabs clients or real results, and never attach invented numbers.
- **Websites** (`websites`): site reviews and fixes that win customers. Never review a real business by name or show a real site: draw a generic mock site in HTML ("Example site"), show the common mistakes, then the fix. Teach principles (speed, clear offer, trust signals, WhatsApp/call buttons, mobile layout).
- **Developer humour** (`humour`): relatable coding and AI moments, told as a swipe story (setup, escalation, punchline on the second-to-last slide). Kind, never punching at people or companies.
- **Behind the build** (`build`): how a tool or workflow is made: agents, pipelines, queues, retries, idempotency, RAG, MCP, evals, observability. Real engineering, explained step by step. Things the owner has actually built (Instahook posting, this content pipeline, Hack Days site) can be described in general terms; no invented metrics.

## Trend radar (search terms for TRENDS.md)
AI model and agent launches, big-tech AI moves, viral AI demos or failures, developer tools trending, AI news India, website or app outages people are talking about (explain the engineering, never mock the company). Angle: "what this means for a business" or "how it actually works".

## Visual system (distinct from Hack Days)
- 1080×1350 JPEG carousel slides (the lab look in surgelabs-lab.md is the current direction for carousels). Background #08080A with soft purple glow (rgba(139,108,255,.22)) top-left and teal glow (rgba(45,212,191,.14)) bottom-right, subtle grain.
- Accents: teal #2DD4BF (lead, key words), purple #8B6CFF (nodes, lines). Cards #111114, lines #232329, muted #8A8A93.
- Type: "Sans" (Inter 600) big tight headlines (letter-spacing -0.035em), key words in teal; "Mono" for diagrams/code; letter-spaced uppercase labels. Brand mark: small glowing teal square + "SURGELABS".
- Minimal, premium, enterprise. No loud colours, heavy gradients or bulky cards.
- Reference: posts/surgelabs/2026-10-06-powering-hackdays.html.

## Stories (two per day, 1080×1920)
- Rotate widely, never the same category two days running: tech news of the day (verified, named source), world tech affairs and big-tech moves, mini explainers (one concept in three lines), SurgeLabs capabilities (agent systems, backend automation, scalable infrastructure: what they solve), automation ideas for businesses, tool or pattern of the day, myth vs fact, Hack Days promotion and benefits (pointing to @homingo.hackdays), teaser of today's feed post.
- Same visual system as the feed (black, teal, purple, tight sans).

## Captions
- Clear, expert, calm. Lead with the insight. Up to 5 hashtags, e.g. #SurgeLabs #AIEngineering #AIAgents #Automation #TechNews. Alt text for every image.
